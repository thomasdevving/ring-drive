# Ring Drive
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
ios
## Stack
Native SwiftUI central client, portable Swift core, ActivityKit/WidgetKit extension and optional local Node webhook relay. The user approved building directly.
## Users
Drivers with Ring cameras at home, and hackathon judges evaluating a native end-to-end demonstration.
## Product Purpose
Triage camera activity into passive or urgent information, explain alerts audibly, help the driver find somewhere to stop and block video until parking is confirmed.
## Operating Context
Amazon Developer Hackathon; Ring Playground/API runtime, iOS 26 Simulator, optional CarPlay Simulator. No Ring account configuration is available yet.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Explicit incident states, inspectable deterministic rules, fault simulation, Apple Maps handoff, conservative stationary evidence. No granted CarPlay entitlement. Interactive fallback must be identified as simulation; official API runtime cannot be certified before credentials are configured.
## Brand Commitments
Ring Drive is a demo concept; no claim of affiliation or official Ring branding. User approved a quiet dark driving view and separate demonstration controls.
## Product Principles
Fail closed for video. Explain evidence without claiming verified identity. Keep driving surfaces brief and auditory. Separate synthetic evidence from official runtime evidence.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Native controls, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels and state indication beyond color.
